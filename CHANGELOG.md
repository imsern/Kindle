# Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta (2026-11-08)

First public beta for World of Warcraft: Forever.

- **Camp bar:** a small movable bar that shows only the camping buttons you need right now.
  - Away from a campfire: place your best campfire kit, or craft one.
  - At a campfire: one button per profession camp object (highest tier you can use) to place or craft it, plus Sit.
  - With camp bonuses and no campfire nearby, the bar stays out of the way.
- **Camp advice:** rates a camp's bonuses for your role (from your class, or pick one) against the bonuses you
  have, including object tiers. Hover the camp's objects so Kindle knows what it has.
- **Reminders:** a sound and message before your camp bonuses run out, and a glow on Sit when sitting would help.
- **Group sharing:** camp info is shared with party/raid members at the same camp who also run Kindle.
- **Settings window** (`/kindle`) with button size, role, reminders and themes: WoW, Dark, Class colour, and
  EllesmereUI / NaowhUI when installed.
- **Welcome guide** on first login.
- Learns your camp recipes automatically when you open a profession window.
